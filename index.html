Paper Explains

How things work, drawn on paper.

Paper Explains makes short vertical videos that explain how everyday things and new technology work: from the fridge and the zipper to face unlock, drone shows and rockets that land themselves. Every video is drawn in a hand-made paper style and comes in English and Arabic.

Paper Explains panel

Paper Explains panel is a private tool that runs only on the channel owner's own computer. It uploads Paper Explains videos to the channel's own YouTube and Instagram accounts and shows the statistics for those videos. It is not offered to anyone else. Read the privacy policy.
