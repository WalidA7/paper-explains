Privacy Policy

Paper Explains panel · last updated 25 October 2026

In short: Paper Explains panel is a private tool used only by the owner of the Paper Explains channel, on their own computer, for their own accounts. It does not collect data about anyone else, and nothing is sold or shared.

What the panel is

Paper Explains panel ("the panel") is a small app that runs locally on the channel owner's computer. It uploads Paper Explains videos to the channel's own YouTube and Instagram accounts, adds the first comment to those videos, and shows statistics for them. Only the channel owner signs in to it. It is not offered to the public.

Data the panel uses

  • YouTube (Google): the channel's name and ID, its videos and their statistics, recent comments on the channel's videos, and permission to upload videos, set thumbnails and post comments as the channel.
  • Instagram (Meta): the account's username, its posts and their statistics (insights), comments on those posts, and permission to publish reels and post comments as the account.
  • TikTok: statistics for the account's own videos.

The panel only ever accesses accounts that belong to Paper Explains. It does not access data about viewers beyond what the platforms show on the channel's own videos (for example the public text of a comment).

Where the data is stored

Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only in files on the channel owner's computer. They are not sent to any server run by Paper Explains. The panel talks directly to the official APIs of Google, Meta and TikTok. To let Instagram fetch a video, a copy of the video is uploaded briefly to Cloudinary and deleted once it has been published.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone, and it is not used for advertising.

Google API Services

The panel's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. TikTok data (statistics for our own videos) is read through TikTok's official API and handled the same way. The panel uses YouTube API Services: see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and for Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites. Deleting the panel's data folder removes everything it has stored.

Contact

Questions: send a message to @paper.explains on Instagram.
